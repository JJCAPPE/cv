## Inventory System Rebuild

Runtime source repository: `inventario-cappellettoshop` at commit `3b2169e6`.

- `scripts/generate-inventory-visuals.mjs` is the deterministic,
  project-local generator for `inventory-cover-exploded.svg`,
  `inventory-decrement-exploded.svg`, and
  `inventory-recovery-exploded.svg`. Its embedded `sourceLedger` maps every
  visible label and connector to the current source path and marks default,
  conditional, recovery, and excluded claims.
- Search and selection labels come from
  `src/components/SearchBar.tsx::searchProducts`,
  `src/components/HomePage.tsx::handleSearchSelect`,
  `src/services/tauri.ts`, and
  `src-tauri/src/products/mod.rs`. They document exact-SKU GraphQL first,
  title-prefix GraphQL fallback, and auto-selection. The selected-product fetch
  and two-location hydration use Shopify REST through
  `src-tauri/src/products/mod.rs::get_product_by_id` and
  `src-tauri/src/inventory/mod.rs::get_inventory_levels_for_locations`.
- Adjustment, status, logging, refresh, undo, and transfer labels come from
  `src/components/HomePage.tsx::handleDecreaseInventory`,
  `handleUndoChange`, and `handleUndoTransfer`;
  `src-tauri/src/inventory/mod.rs::decrease_inventory_with_logging`,
  `undo_decrease_inventory_with_logging`,
  `transfer_inventory_between_locations`,
  `has_zero_inventory_across_all_locations`, and `update_product_status`; and
  `src-tauri/src/firebase/mod.rs::FirebaseClient.create_log`.
- The frontend/Rust and location boundaries come from
  `src-tauri/src/main.rs::tauri::generate_handler!`,
  `src-tauri/src/location/mod.rs`, and `src-tauri/src/utils/mod.rs::AppConfig`.
  Published diagrams use generic primary/secondary labels and contain no
  configured IDs, store identifiers, credentials, customer data, or stock
  values.
- The generated SVGs are source-grounded explanatory artifacts, not runtime
  traces or performance measurements. Solid connectors are the default path;
  dashed connectors are fallback, conditional, or recovery behavior. The
  default decrement is sequential and non-atomic. Transfer rollback covers a
  failed destination adjustment, while later Firestore logging warnings do not
  roll back successful Shopify writes.
- The manual comparison lane follows Shopify’s official
  [Adjusting inventory quantities](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/inventory/adjusting-inventory/adjusting-inventory-quantities)
  help page, specifically the desktop Inventory-page removal workflow,
  accessed 2026-07-29. The known-SKU lookup is added as the matched task’s
  common first stage.
- `src/content/inventoryWorkflowBenchmark.json` records the matched task,
  source URLs, safe-test protocol, publication gate, and raw-sample slots. Its
  current status is `workflow-only`: no live inventory mutation was
  authorized, no timing samples are present, and the site publishes no
  elapsed-time, percentage-reduction, or faster-than claim.
- The earlier `70%`, `80%`, `60%`, and `43%` performance claims have no
  benchmark artifact in the inspected source trees or history and are
  excluded.
