# Resume human-review scorecard

Use this scorecard after the deterministic PDF and ATS gates pass. Three reviewers with technical-recruiting or engineering-hiring experience should evaluate the same final PDF independently.

## Ten-second scan

Show the resume for ten seconds, hide it, and ask the reviewer to answer from memory:

1. What kind of engineer is this candidate?
2. What is the strongest experience?
3. What is one measurable result?
4. What is one project or research area?
5. When does the candidate graduate?

### Completion gate

- 3/3 reviewers identify software/AI or applied-ML engineering as the primary profile.
- 3/3 identify May 2028 graduation.
- At least 2/3 remember Banca Mediolanum.
- At least 2/3 remember one quantified result.
- At least 2/3 remember one selected project.

Record any claim that appears unsupported, unclear, overstated, confidential, or difficult to explain in an interview.

### Completion gate

- Mean score is at least 4.0/5 in every dimension.
- No reviewer marks a claim as implausible or unsupported.
- No reviewer reports that the Banca section is incomprehensible because of jargon.
- No reviewer is uncertain about what the candidate personally built.
- At least 2/3 would retain the resume for full technical review for a matching internship.
