# Maven AGI source registry

**Research snapshot:** 22 August 2026  
**Purpose:** support the claims in [`README.md`](./README.md) with a dated, auditable source ledger.  
**Source policy:** official Maven product pages, official job descriptions, official/public GitHub repositories, and Giacomo's source repositories are primary. Public professional profiles are used only for identity, role, education, and location-path evidence. Third-party sources are used only to map open-source overlap or corroborate context, never to assert a Maven internal problem.

A live page marked **accessed 2026-08-22** may change after the research snapshot. Publication dates below are the page dates visible during the research pass; where a page was evergreen or a job posting did not expose a reliable publication date, the access date is used and the uncertainty is stated.

## Source exclusions and cautions

The final report deliberately does **not** rely on:

- scraped employee databases for email addresses;
- inferred LinkedIn mutual connections not visible in a connected source;
- a shared former employer as proof of a warm relationship;
- unverified claims that Brian Kulis knows Sami Shalabi or another Maven employee;
- company-review sites as evidence of technical priorities;
- a claim that Maven lacks release-safety, replay, integration-test, or observability infrastructure;
- unsourced revenue, valuation, or customer-count claims as a reason to approach the company;
- private Banca Mediolanum code or customer data.
