## Excluded evidence

The audit deliberately excludes:

- scraped personal contact details;
- generic salary pages when an official role page exists;
- search snippets that conflict with a live structured header;
- mirror identifiers represented as official GE Vernova job IDs;
- public titles represented as evidence of Giacomo’s relationship;
- claims about proprietary GE Vernova data, code, models, customers, internal reporting lines, hiring managers or unpublished immigration policy.

## Interpretation rules

1. Live official role pages control title, location, `R...` ID and structured posting dates.
2. Body/header inconsistencies are preserved rather than silently reconciled.
3. Historical role scope may be reconstructed from multiple mirrors, but exact dates and IDs remain unknown unless a first-party source survives.
4. Public availability is not assumed to grant unrestricted redistribution; ISO-NE raw-data publication remains gated on terms confirmation.
5. Model code license and model-card/weight terms are checked separately before implementation.
6. Candidate-source claims are limited to what is documented in the résumé and accessible repositories.
